# IDEBP privacy notice — production draft

14 October 2026. **For review; do not publish this as the live-service notice until the items below are resolved.** The preview has its own accurate privacy page and does not accept accounts or subscriptions.

## Who is responsible

IDEBP is being developed and operated by Nathan Tracey. Contact: ntracey@gmail.com. If a company becomes the operator, insert its legal name and contact details and assess any transfer of account data before changing this notice.

## Information and purposes

We plan to process account email and authentication details to provide optional membership and source-management access; profile and organisational details to run the public directory; ownership evidence to check management requests; saved sources and recommendations to deliver member functions; consent/preferences to provide optional newsletters; and limited technical/security records to maintain the service. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider; plaintext passwords must not be logged or retained by IDEBP.

Browsing does not require an account. Registration emails and verification documents remain private. Public profile names, logos, descriptions, declared research languages, website/social links and explicitly selected contact details can be viewed by anyone. A representative must have permission to publish another person's contact details.

## Proposed lawful-basis decisions

Confirm and record these decisions before collection. Account delivery may rely on contract where processing is necessary for the requested service. Directory administration, ownership checks, public professional contact information and security may rely on legitimate interests following a documented assessment. Newsletter marketing uses a separate consent process. These are design proposals, not a finding that every use is automatically lawful. Do not treat agreement to this notice as blanket consent.

## Sources and recipients

Account holders provide most information. Editors may add public organisational information from the organisation's own website and relevant official sources. Record the original URL and date. Provide any required notice when adding personal information from elsewhere.

Expected service providers are Cloudflare for delivery/security and Supabase for database/authentication/storage. Add the selected email provider and any social scheduling provider, actual service purposes and applicable contracts. Published directory data is visible internationally. Third-party websites have their own notices and practices.

## Proposed retention schedule — confirm operationally

Unconfirmed account/profile drafts: 30 days. Closed member accounts and private saved collections: delete within 30 days, subject to narrow documented exceptions. Ownership evidence: retain the decision record; minimise and delete supporting documents when no longer needed, proposed within 90 days after resolution. Security logs: proposed 30 days. Admin audit history: proposed 12 months. Newsletter suppression: retain only the minimum needed to respect the opt-out. Backups: insert actual provider schedules and deletion limitations. Public organisational listings may continue after a manager closes their personal account, with personal contact details removed or rechecked.

These are proposed limits, not claims about configured settings. Implement scheduled deletion and verify the chosen providers' capabilities before publishing promises.

## International processing

Choose the database region, list actual processing locations, examine processor terms and document any relevant transfer mechanism. A UK/EU database region does not by itself mean every provider's support or infrastructure processing is confined there. Insert the final safeguards and how a person can obtain information about them.

## Preferences, cookies and storage

The preview stores language, saved-source IDs and demonstration recommendations in browser storage only when used; a clear-data control removes them. Production will additionally need authenticated sessions. Inventory the precise cookies/storage and purposes before launch. Optional analytics or marketing technologies need a separate decision and appropriate controls; the current preview adds neither.

## Your choices and rights

Provide routes to access and correct personal information, close an account, request deletion or restriction, exercise applicable portability rights, object where applicable, and withdraw newsletter consent. Explain relevant limits in plain language. Contact Nathan using the address above. People may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ or their relevant local authority.

The directory's ordering uses source-level information and eligible member recommendations. Explain the ranking factors separately. It does not make operational policing decisions about individuals.

## Before publication

Confirm controller identity; lawful bases and assessments; processor contracts; data region/transfers; deployed retention schedules; exact cookies/storage; rights request handling; account deletion; breach procedure; newsletter consent and suppression; actual provider names; and translated notice review. Add an effective date and revision history. Review new requirements if the business model changes.
