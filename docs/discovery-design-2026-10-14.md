# id-ebp identity and discovery revision

The wordmark is now lowercase **id-ebp**, with a horizontal hyphen matching the domain. Its vector mark combines a half-globe arc with separate nodes and curved connections converging into one horizontal direction. The favicon uses the same artwork.

The hero photograph fades out along its left edge. The floating knowledge-sharing bubble and its translations were removed. The society non-partnership sentence was removed from the homepage.

The homepage promotes the live catalogue totals in a large navy feature. Source categories offer working directory filters and selected profile links: academic research, universities, EBP societies, blogs and practitioner voices, researchers who write, and independent institutes. Categories can overlap. University and Researcher are explicit classifications; the commercial Cambridge Centre is not classified as a university.

Country browsing shows 30 labelled flag tiles, each with its source count. Tiles filter by the organisation’s home country, distinct from geographical coverage. Unicode flag icons are accompanied by country names. Counts derive from visible catalogue records rather than hardcoded promotional totals.
