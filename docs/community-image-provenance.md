## Collaboration prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide landscape website photograph-style illustration for an independent international evidence-based policing directory. Create one warm, credible documentary-style scene in a bright British community centre: a small group of diverse adult residents and two approachable British neighbourhood police officers in simple white short-sleeve shirts with subtle black shoulder epaulettes, seated as equals around a wooden table with a female university researcher, reviewing printed research charts and a neighbourhood map, listening and discussing. Friendly, thoughtful collaboration, natural candid expressions, no posed handshake. The officers wear no hats, no body armour, no high visibility jackets, no visible weapons, no equipment belts. Plants and tall windows, everyday British community setting. Landscape 3:2, medium-wide composition, warm daylight, navy and soft teal clothing accents, realistic anatomy and hands, restrained premium editorial photography. All people fictional. No identifiable organisation, no insignia or readable lettering, no logos, no watermark. Essential constraint: community engagement and shared learning only; absolutely no public-order policing, tactical or operational activity, arrests, protests, emergency vehicles or confrontation. The scene will be captioned AI-generated illustration on the website.

## Community engagement prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide landscape editorial photograph-style illustration for a community-minded evidence-based policing website. Fictional candid scene on a welcoming British high street beside a small community café: an approachable female British neighbourhood officer in a plain white short-sleeve shirt with black epaulettes and dark trousers (no vest, no hat, no equipment belt or weapons), chatting with a middle-aged shopkeeper at the café doorway and a young adult resident with a bicycle. Everyday relaxed listening, all standing on equal footing, natural smiles, authentic lived-in surroundings, subtle plants and brickwork, pale blue daylight with warm café light, premium documentary photography, wide 3:2 composition. Scene solely community engagement. No police cars, no public order, no tactical or operational policing, no crowd control, no arrests, no conflict, no emergency or distress. No real people, no recognisable force insignia, no readable text, no logos, no watermark. The site will label this AI-generated illustration.
